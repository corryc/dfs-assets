Refinancing questions

Doncaster refinancing, common questions

When should I refinance my mortgage in Doncaster? +

If you haven't reviewed your rate in the last 12 months, you may be paying more than necessary. A refinance may be worth considering if rates have fallen or if your circumstances have changed, such as improved credit, higher income, or more equity, which could unlock better terms.

How much could I save by refinancing? +

Potential benefit depends on your current rate, loan amount, remaining term, and individual circumstances. For example, a 0.2% interest rate reduction on an $800,000 loan could result in approximately $1,600 annual interest savings, subject to eligibility and your circumstances. A broker compares your existing loan against 30+ lenders to explore what options may be available to you.

Is there a cost to refinance? +

Refinancing typically involves discharge fees (around $100-$300) and new establishment fees from your new lender. A broker helps you understand these costs upfront and calculates whether the interest savings justify the fees, usually within 12 months.

How long does refinancing take in Doncaster? +

With Digital Finance Solutions, you can get pre-approval within 24 to 72 hours. The full refinancing process typically takes 4 to 6 weeks from application to settlement, depending on documentation and lender processing times.

Can I refinance if I have bad credit? +

Yes. While major banks may decline you, specialist lenders may consider refinancing even with a poor credit history, especially if your circumstances have since improved. We have access to 30+ lenders with varying criteria.

Will refinancing affect my credit score? +

Refinancing does involve a credit inquiry, which may cause a small, temporary impact on your score. However, reducing your interest rate and demonstrating responsible repayment usually improves your score over time, subject to your individual circumstances.

Do I need to use the same bank? +

No. Refinancing means switching to a different lender for a potentially better rate and terms. A broker compares across 30+ lenders, major banks and non-bank options, to find what suits your situation best.
